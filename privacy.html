Joma: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · বাংলা

Joma ("the app", package app.joma.payments) keeps a record of the mobile-wallet payments you receive. In this policy "we" means the developer of Joma, as named on the app's Google Play listing. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it keeps and what you can control. Contact: https://github.com/cmtboy/joma/issues.

What the app accesses

With your permission, the app receives new incoming text messages (SMS). It processes only messages from bKash, Nagad, Rocket (16216) and Upay. Messages from any other sender are ignored and never read, stored or sent.

What the app keeps

From those senders, the app keeps a message only if it is a notice that you received money and contains an amount and a transaction ID (TrxID). For each kept payment it stores the wallet name, amount, TrxID, the sender's phone number, the time, and the message text. Other messages from those four senders (offers, balance alerts, payments you sent, one-time codes) are discarded immediately and never stored.

These records are stored on your phone.

Optional online backup

If you turn on Online backup in Settings and enter a server address and API key, the app sends your kept payment records (the items above) over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the server you entered. That server is chosen and controlled by you or your business; we do not receive this data. It happens only after you turn it on and only for payments received after that. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. Backup is off by default. How the server you entered stores and uses the data is governed by its operator, not by this policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting services.
  • The app cannot send SMS, cannot access your wallet, PIN or balance, and does not read your SMS inbox history, contacts or other apps.
  • The app needs no account or sign-up.

Permissions

  • Receive SMS: to see new payment messages from the four wallets.
  • Notifications (optional): to warn you if tracking stops.
  • Internet: used only for the optional Online backup.
  • Run at startup / background: so tracking resumes after the phone restarts.

Security

Backup credentials are stored encrypted on your phone. Data sent for backup uses HTTPS. We limit what the app collects to what it needs to work.

Your choices and data deletion

  • On your phone: open Settings, then Delete all records, or uninstall the app. This removes every record stored on the device.
  • Revoke access: remove the SMS permission in your phone's app settings; tracking stops.
  • Backup data: turn off Online backup. Records already sent are held by the server you entered; ask that server's operator to delete them. We do not hold a copy.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: https://github.com/cmtboy/joma/issues.

Joma is an independent tool and is not affiliated with bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay.
